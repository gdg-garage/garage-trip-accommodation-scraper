This document establishes the official technical analysis of **Chalupa Nový Svět** (`e-chalupy 3649`, Prostřední Lipka) as the retreat group's gold-standard benchmark, and details how the top contenders from the filtered queue compare across critical real-world retreat dimensions.

The retreat group has spent **4 vacations** in the Orlické hory valley around Králíky and Prostřední Lipka. ## 4. In-Depth Topic Breakdown

### A. Bedrooms & Bed Density
- **The Nový Svět Advantage**: Nový Svět's 14 bedrooms mean almost everyone sleeps in pairs (1 double or 2 twin beds). Only two rooms have a 3rd extra bed. There are zero 4-to-6 person bunk dorms.
- **The Challenger Tradeoff**:
  - *Chata U Mazliků* comes closest with **11 bedrooms** configured as double and quad suites with private doors and separate en-suite bathrooms.
  - *Lachov 14* matches the scale with **13 bedrooms**, but several rooms have 4 beds.
  - *Chata Termix* has 10 rooms, but reaches 46 beds through heavy bunk bed usage.
  - *Statek Tylov* and *Vlětice* (8 bedrooms) rely on sharing double beds and 4-bed attic rooms.

### B. Bathroom Density & Morning Rush
- **1st Place**: **Chata Termix** (`10 bathrooms` — 100% private en-suite in every bedroom).
- **2nd Place**: **Chata U Mazliků** (`7 full bathrooms` — one dedicated bathroom per apartment).
- **3rd Place**: **Chalupa Nový Svět** (`6 bathrooms + 7 WCs` — steady 1:2 room ratio).
- **Bottleneck Caution**: **Statek Tylov** (`4 bathrooms for 31 people` — ~7.7 people per shower) and **Chalupa Měšťan** (`4 bathrooms for 27 people`).

### C. Chair Ergonomics & Workstations
- **Best Unified Hall**: **Chata Termix** (45 solid wooden chairs) and **Chalupa Nový Svět** (30+ padded dining chairs). All 25+ developers can code simultaneously in a single bright room.
- **Distributed Layout**: **Chata U Mazliků** (16 dining chairs + 8 glazed veranda chairs + 10 lower gym chairs). Better acoustic isolation for Zoom calls, but divides the group into two clusters.
- **Ergonomic Caution**: *Statek Vlětice* uses bistro chairs in the vaulted hall (less ergonomic for 8-hour hacking); *Statek Razová* was disqualified for wooden trojlavice benches.

### D. Separate Rooms for Board Games / Werewolf
- **Best Acoustic Separation**:
  1. **Lachov 14**: Two completely standalone halls on separate floors with massive stone/brick floor separation.
  2. **Statek U Pepíka**: Underground vaulted wine cellar — zero sound escapes to the bedrooms above.
  3. **Statek Vlětice**: Two independent standalone event barns (horní & dolní stodola) across the courtyard.
  4. **Chalupa Nový Svět**: Dedicated upper mezzanine lounge with closing doors.
  5. **Chata U Mazliků**: Dedicated lower-level basement gym / games room.

### E. Kitchen Infrastructure & Refrigeration
- **Nový Svět Benchmark**: **4 full-size refrigerators**, commercial pass-through dishwasher (3-minute cycle), double induction/electric stoves. Unmatched convenience for large food/beer storage.
- **Gastro / Hotel Rivals**: **Chata Termix** (walk-in cold room, commercial stainless prep, industrial dishwashers) and **Statek Vlětice** (commercial catering prep kitchen).
- **Domestic Challenge**: Smaller chalets (*Tylov*, *Hříbek*, *Medunka*) have only 2 standard domestic refrigerators, which requires rotating beer cans or using cold outdoor storage.

In **Simulation Run 10 (The Nový Svět Benchmark Showdown)**:

### The Strategic Recommendation:
1. **Choose Chata U Mazliků (39,900 CZK)** IF the group wants to:
   - Save **40,000 CZK in cash** (which easily covers all team catering, steaks, and craft beer kegs for the entire week).
   - Explore fresh mountain scenery in **Jizerské hory** with brand-new trails and singletracks.
   - Enjoy pristine **9.5/10 Scandinavian renovation** with **11 bedrooms and 7 en-suite bathrooms**.
   - Bring dogs (pet friendly).
2. **Choose Chalupa Nový Svět (~80,000 CZK)** IF:
   - Having all 25–30 hackers in **one single room** with 30 padded chairs is a strict non-negotiable requirement.
   - The team refuses to compromise on **14 individual bedrooms** and **4 refrigerators**.
   - The team does not care about returning to the **same Orlické hory valley for the 5th time** and does not care about having a swimming pool or sauna.
